# Privacy Policy — AI Writing Assistant

**Last updated:** July 5, 2026

## Overview

AI Writing Assistant is a Chrome extension that helps you write better text on any webpage. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection

**We do not operate any servers.** All data stays on your device except when you explicitly send text to an AI provider.

### Local Storage
- API keys for AI providers (encrypted by Chrome)
- Privacy settings (allowlist, blocklist, personal dictionary)
- Local analytics (suggestion counts, acceptance rate)

### AI Provider Communication
When you trigger a writing action, selected text is sent to your configured AI provider (OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Anthropic) to generate suggestions. We do not intercept, store, or log this data.

### On-Device Mode
When enabled, the extension uses Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano LanguageModel API. No text is sent to cloud servers.

## Data We Do NOT Collect
- Browsing history
- Passwords or form data (except text you explicitly select)
- Personal identifiers
- Telemetry or usage analytics sent to third parties

## Third-Party Services
Your use of AI providers is governed by their respective privacy policies:
- [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Anthropic Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy)
